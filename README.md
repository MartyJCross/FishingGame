# Longwater (working title)

A 2D side-view fishing game with physically simulated rods, lines, reels and water, and hundreds of real fish
species that behave, feed and fight like they do in nature. You play the new survey angler of a broke river
trust and fish your way from a farm pond to the deep ocean, restoring a damaged river system and following a
missing keeper's logbook to a mystery called the Lantern.

**Status:** design and build plan complete; development starts at Milestone M0.
